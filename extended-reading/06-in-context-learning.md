# Extended Reading — 6. In-Context Learning & Attention Circuits

Papers judged on-topic and reasonably solid, but that didn't make `README.md`'s curated top-10 for this section — usually because they're recent, unreviewed preprints without a track record yet, or cover narrower/more applied ground than the core list, or are largely redundant with a stronger pick already in the main table. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how the split against the main table works. Promote an entry to `README.md` if it proves out.

| Paper | Authors | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Induction in Both Directions: A Mechanistic Analysis of In-Context Learning in Masked Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15893) | Catruna, Radoi | 2026 | Extends induction-head analysis to diffusion language models — a genuinely different architecture family; exploratory but promising. |
| [Adaptive inference and function vectors in deep transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16694) | Raj, Reddy | 2026 | Another function-vector extension; vaguer on its specific novel claim than the pick that made README.md. |
| [Where Computation Lives Inside TabPFN: Causal Localisation of Attention Head Function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12917) | Gupta, Kumar, Mandal, Deshpande | 2026 | First causal mechanistic analysis of a tabular foundation model; interesting "first of its kind" claim in a domain outside language models. |
| [When Do Attention Circuits Form? Developmental Trajectories of Capability and Attention-Sink Emergence Across Three 1B-Class Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02378) | Xu | 2026 | Overlaps thematically with Phase Transitions in Attention (README.md); single-author and less theoretically developed. |
| [The Ignition Index: Measuring Global Workspace Dynamics in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05160) | Rahbar | 2026 | Applies Global Workspace Theory (a consciousness-science framework) to transformer internals; speculative framing relative to the rest of this list. |
| [Fast & Faithful Function Vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05079) | Pham, Segeler, Wiegand, Samek, Lapuschkin, et al. | 2026 | An efficiency-focused improvement to function-vector construction; useful but incremental next to the causal-decomposition pick in README.md. |
| [Function-Vector Heads Are Two Populations: Writers and Cancellers in In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07560) | Wang | 2026 | Finds substructure within function-vector heads; a specific, falsifiable claim worth tracking for independent replication. |
| [In-Context Learning Operates as Concept Subspace Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18830) | Tang, Jiang, Karray, Hu | 2026 | A unifying subspace-learning reframing of ICL; covers similar ground to the geometry-reorganization pick in README.md from a more conceptual angle. |
| [Test, then Route: How Language Models Execute In-Context Conditional Rules Across Models and Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04183) | Thavarasa, Sukumar | 2026 | Probes whether models implement a predicate-then-route "circuit" for conditional rules; suggestive rather than definitive. |
| [Grounding latent algorithm routing in transformer reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24471) | Zhang, Ma | 2026 | Studies whether transformers organize episode-level adaptation around distinct inductive-bias families — relevant to What Learning Algorithm Is In-Context Learning? (README.md), exploratory. |
