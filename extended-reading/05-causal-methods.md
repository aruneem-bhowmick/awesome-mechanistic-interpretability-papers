# Extended Reading — 5. Causal Methods: Patching, Ablation, Editing

Papers judged on-topic and reasonably solid, but that didn't make `README.md`'s curated top-10 for this section — usually because they're recent, unreviewed preprints without a track record yet, or cover narrower/more applied ground than the core list. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how the split against the main table works. Promote an entry to `README.md` if it proves out.

| Paper | Authors | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A Six-Dimensional Taxonomy of Post-Training Adaptation Techniques with Applications in AI Governance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06246) | Afdideh, Seoane, Abtahi | 2026 | A governance-oriented taxonomy that includes model editing among post-training techniques; useful reference framing, not a research contribution to causal methods itself. |
| [Suppression Sticks, Locality Is Fragile: A Closed-Loop Target-and-Control Audit of Task-Vector Negation in VLA Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04692) | Wang, Guo, Dai, Zhao, Guo, et al. | 2026 | Extends task-vector-editing questions (in the spirit of MEMIT/ROME) to robot-control policies; niche domain, solid audit methodology. |
| [A Theory of Conditional Collapse under Low-Rank Weight-Space Ablations: I. The Single-Block Theory and Synthetic Validation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03620) | Khemais | 2026 | Dense single-author theoretical treatment of when weight-space ablation and activation patching should agree; a companion paper on arXiv extends this further but is redundant to list separately here. |
| [Divergent large language model predictions from convergent representations in ambiguous word pairs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01816) | Scott, Pat, Liesaputra | 2026 | Layer-by-layer analysis of lexical ambiguity resolution across model scales; more representation-geometry (§4) flavored than a causal-intervention study, but surfaced here. |
| [Bridging the English-Arabic Medical Knowledge Gap: Targeted Low-Rank Adaptation via Causal Layer Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00207) | Abouzahir, Khan, Ali-Hassan, Ma, Saleh, et al. | 2026 | Causal layer selection used in service of a cross-lingual medical fine-tuning gap; the interpretability angle is a means to an applied end. |
| [Evidence-Type Competition: When Can Interventional Data Teach Language Models Causal Direction?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29484) | Xun | 2026 | About whether interventional training data teaches models to reason causally — LLM causal-reasoning *capability*, not causal methods applied to interpret a model. |
| [Token-Level Diagnosis of Sycophancy in LLMs with Attribution-Guided Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28906) | Nguyen, Kamruzzaman, Chhabra, Kim | 2026 | Solid applied steering-for-diagnosis case study on sycophancy; narrower than the steering-methodology papers already in README.md. |
| [Minimizing Targeted Activations: Input-Only Suppression of Evaluation-Awareness Latents in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25907) | Mody, Agarwal, Mittal, Mahato | 2026 | Novel input-side dual of activation steering, aimed at a single specific latent (evaluation-awareness); narrow scope so far. |
| [Context Is King: How In-Context Specification Shapes the Geometry of Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24425) | David, Fomin | 2026 | Studies how in-context specification reshapes concept geometry — connects to Not All Language Model Features Are Linear (§4) more than to patching/editing specifically. |
