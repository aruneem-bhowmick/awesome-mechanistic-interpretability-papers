# Extended Reading — 3. Superposition & Sparse Autoencoders

Papers judged on-topic and reasonably solid, but that didn't make `README.md`'s curated top-10 for this section — usually because they're recent, unreviewed preprints without a track record yet, or cover narrower/more applied ground than the core list. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how the split against the main table works. Promote an entry to `README.md` if it proves out.

| Paper | Authors | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Finding Usable Weight Mechanisms with Tiled SVD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06969) | Manvi, Tajreen | 2026 | A weight-based alternative to activation-based SAE atlases; competes with rather than extends the current README lineup. |
| [Bias Analysis of L2 Speaking Assessment Systems Using Concept Activation Vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06300) | Labroo, Qian, Knill | 2026 | Applied fairness audit via concept activation vectors; domain-specific (speech scoring), not about superposition/SAEs directly. |
| [CircuitSteer: Geometrically Aligned Multi-Layer Steering via Sparse Autoencoder Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05732) | Saadatinia, Razmara, Aryashad, Abbasi, Azizi | 2026 | Combines SAE features with multi-layer steering; incremental combination of known techniques. |
| [Strengthening Target-Language Features: SAE-Based Steering for Multilingual Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04904) | Wang, Koehn | 2026 | Applied SAE steering for multilingual behavior; solid applied case study rather than a methodological contribution. |
| [Beyond Routing Weights: Faithful Response-Level Interpretation of Mixture-of-Experts Reward Models via Contribution Contrast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06400) | Wang, Mu, Jobanputra, Wang, Oh, et al. | 2026 | Interpretability for MoE reward models specifically; narrower application domain (RLHF reward modeling). |
| [ECG-InterpBench: Benchmarking the Interpretability of ECG Foundation Models with Matched-Scale Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27404) | Duan, Qiu | 2026 | Shows SAE methodology transferring to a medical (ECG) domain; useful pointer, not core LM interpretability. |
| [From Found to Designed: Concepts as a Design Axis for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26825) | Shani | 2026 | Position-piece arguing for engineering concept structure rather than discovering it post hoc; thought-provoking counterpoint to the SAE-discovery paradigm, but single-author and light on empirics so far. |
| [Interpretable GOHR Agents via Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25132) | Tan, Zhao, Qin, Wang, Feldman, et al. | 2026 | SAEs applied to interpret an RL agent rather than a language model; distinct domain, narrow case study. |
| [Sparse Autoencoders Encode Both Concepts and Functions: The Downstream Geometry of Feature Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24645) | Hoang, Chatterjee, Chakraborty, Gurevych, Dutta | 2026 | Directly relevant to the feature-to-behavior link question already covered by Identifying Functionally Important Features; good but didn't clear the bar over the two picks that did. |
| [Toward Mechanistic Interpretability of an AI Foundation Model Fine-Tuned for Atmospheric Chemistry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20778) | Hu, Higuera-Mendieta, Sturm, Kelp | 2026 | Cross-domain application of mech-interp framing to a scientific foundation model; niche. |
| [Scaling Interpretable Transformers with Parity Bottleneck Layers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20652) | Mack, Tou, Henry, Wu, Greenspan | 2026 | An interpretable-by-design architectural alternative to post-hoc SAEs — a genuinely different research direction worth tracking, but early and unproven relative to the SAE mainstream covered in README.md. |
| [Causal dictionary learning reveals and validates transcription-factor binding features in genomic language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19618) | Ali | 2026 | Dictionary learning applied to genomic language models; cross-domain pointer, narrow audience. |
