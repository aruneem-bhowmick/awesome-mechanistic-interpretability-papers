# Extended Reading — 2. Circuits & Features

Papers judged on-topic and reasonably solid, but that didn't make `README.md`'s curated top-10 for this section — usually because they're recent, unreviewed preprints without a track record yet, or cover narrower/more applied ground than the core list. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how the split against the main table works. Promote an entry to `README.md` if it proves out.

| Paper | Authors | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Where Animacy Lives in Large Language Models: Tracing the Circuits of the Animacy Concept](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20995) | Punzo, Cinà, Pezzelle | 2026 | A single-concept circuit case study in the mold of IOI, narrower in scope. |
| [CircuitKIT: Circuit Discovery, Evaluation, and Application Toolkit for Mechanistic Interpretability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19317) | Seth, Gosalia, Kasliwal, Sankarapu | 2026 | A toolkit paper more than a research finding — arguably belongs alongside TransformerLens/CircuitsVis in §11 once vetted. |
| [Cost Accounting for Reactive Computational Graphs: Exhaustive Sweeps, Sequential Mutation, and the Backward-Locality Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18323) | Khemais | 2026 | Dense, theoretical treatment of the compute cost of patching sweeps; single-author preprint, unclear traction yet. |
| [Transcoders for Investigating Deception in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14791) | Lim, Leow, Chia | 2026 | Applies transcoder-based circuit tracing to a safety-relevant behavior (deception); early-stage applied case study. |
| [Mechanistic Interpretability for Neural Networks: Circuits, Sparse Features and Symbolic Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07316) | Sawant, Krejčí | 2026 | Reads more like a survey than a §2-specific contribution; compare against §10's survey entries. |
| [Symbolic Mechanistic Data Attribution: Tracing Training Influence to Learned Behavioral Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29171) | Habibi, Lee, El-Nasr | 2026 | Connects circuits to training-data attribution — adjacent to §2 rather than squarely in it. |
| [Circuit Tracing in Autoregressive Protein Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16044) | Tsui, Deinzer, Saeedi, Aghazadeh | 2026 | Shows circuit-tracing methodology transferring outside natural-language models; niche domain. |
| [DifFRACT: Diffusion Feature Reconstruction and Attribution for Circuit Tracing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15796) | Mazur, Konovalova, Alanov | 2026 | Transcoder-based circuit tracing for diffusion (image) models rather than language models. |
| [How Many Counterfactuals Does It Take? Probing VLM Hallucinations Through Circuits and Causal Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08777) | Gupta, Singh, Sinha, Modi, Tomar | 2026 | Applied circuit + causal analysis of vision-language hallucination; solid but narrow application. |
| [Shared Semantics, Divergent Mechanisms: Unsupervised Feature Discovery by Aligning Semantics and Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08236) | Cho, Roh, Kim | 2026 | Mechanism-auditing method via feature discovery; incremental relative to what's already in §2/§3. |
| [Every Component is a Lookup: Token Attribution and Composition from a Single Decomposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23393) | Chen, Plaat, Stein | 2026 | A unified attention+MLP attribution scheme; worth tracking if it gets independent validation. |
