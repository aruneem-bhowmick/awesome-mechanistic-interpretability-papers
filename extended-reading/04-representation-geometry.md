# Extended Reading — 4. Representation Geometry & Linear Structure

Papers judged on-topic and reasonably solid, but that didn't make `README.md`'s curated top-10 for this section — usually because they're recent, unreviewed preprints without a track record yet, or cover narrower/more applied ground than the core list. See [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how the split against the main table works. Promote an entry to `README.md` if it proves out.

See `PAPER-INBOX.md`'s triage history / issue #3 for the query-tuning follow-up this motivates.

| Paper | Authors | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Measuring Concept Content in Text from LLM Activations: ESG Evidence from Concept Vectors and Linear Probes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07208) | Hazenoot, Ren, Zohrehvand | 2026 | Concept vectors + linear probes applied to ESG/finance text; the method is on-topic, the application is niche. |
| [MI-MIDI: Mechanistic Interpretability of Text-to-MIDI Generation Models via Probing, Lenses and Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06638) | Poćwiardowski, Modrzejewski | 2026 | Probing/lens/steering methodology transferred to symbolic music generation — a legitimate cross-domain application, distinct from the language-model focus of README.md's core list. |
| [Divergent Response Modes in Frontier Language Models Under Steering Pressure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06578) | Jalal-Kamali | 2026 | Compares how different frontier models respond to steering pressure; single-author preprint, vague enough on methodology to warrant waiting for follow-up. |
| [Cloud-ScPO: Hidden-State Geometry for Semi-Supervised Preference Optimization in LLM Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01014) | Liu, Hu | 2026 | Uses hidden-state geometry as a tool for a training method (semi-supervised preference optimization) rather than studying the geometry itself. |
| [Your Agentic LLMs Secretly Encode Latent Signals of Indirect Prompt-Injection Exposure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02657) | Dong, Liu, Zhang, Deng, Peng, et al. | 2026 | A concrete "is X internally represented" finding with real safety relevance, but scoped narrowly to agentic prompt-injection exposure. |
| [Mobius Learning: Cyclic Depth Folding in Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17843) | Zhu | 2026 | Challenges the standard view that shallow/deep transformer blocks have fixed, distinct representational roles; interesting claim, single-author and not yet independently validated. |
